## GPU settings for JAX (MJX)

- `JAX_DEFAULT_MATMUL_PRECISION=highest`: required on Ampere (RTX 30-series)
  GPUs by the MuJoCo Playground README (Requirements 0.10 and 1.5). Every MJX
  smoke, training, and evaluation command exports it.
- `XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false`: under WSL2 a single multi-GB
  `cudaMalloc` fails with `CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY` even when `nvidia-smi`
  reports ~7.9 GB free (seen in the first smoke dry run: 6.0, 5.0, 4.5 and
  4.05 GiB preallocations all failed). Allocating on demand avoids that.

## Smoke-test environments

Created by `scripts/make_smoke_venvs.sh` (on the Linux filesystem):

- `~/.venvs/lo-mjx`: MuJoCo Playground + MJX + JAX CUDA 12; frozen in
  `docs/evidence/freeze_lo-mjx.txt`.
- `~/.venvs/lo-mjlab`: mjlab + MuJoCo Warp + PyTorch; frozen in
  `docs/evidence/freeze_lo-mjlab.txt`. mjlab 1.6.0 pins `mujoco~=3.11.0` and
  `mujoco-warp~=3.11.0`. torch 2.14.1 from PyPI is the CUDA 13.0 build
  (`nvidia-cuda-runtime==13.0.96` in the freeze), not cu128.

Findings that shape the environment design:

- MJX-JAX 3.16.0 ray casting (`mujoco/mjx/_src/ray.py`, `_RAY_FUNC`) covers
  plane, sphere, capsule, ellipsoid, box, and mesh, but not cylinder:
  cylinder geoms are silently invisible to MJX rangefinders. Round obstacles are
  therefore upright capsules, whose straight section has a cylinder's
  cross-section at ray height.
- MJX 3.16.0 rangefinders index `mat_rgba`, so a model with no materials fails
  inside `mjx.forward`; every model defines at least one material.
- MJX and MuJoCo rangefinders exclude geoms on the ray site's own body
  (`bodyexclude = site body`), and skip geoms whose rgba alpha is 0.
